1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
-------------------
ACME Corp is a mid-size technology and consumer goods company founded in 2010. The company operates across three main verticals: Electronics, Clothing, and Books. With over 200 registered customers and a sales force of 50 employees, ACME generates revenue primarily through direct B2C and B2B sales channels. Headquarters are located in San Francisco, CA, with regional offices in New York and Chicago.

3. SALES POLICIES & GUIDELINES
--------------------------------

3.1 Discount Authority
Sales representatives may apply discounts within the following limits:
- Sales Rep: up to 10% discount, no approval needed.
- Senior Sales Rep: up to 15% discount, no approval needed.
- Sales Manager: up to 25% discount, no approval needed.
- VP of Sales: up to 40% discount; CEO sign-off required for discounts above 35%.

3.2 Return & Refund Policy
- Electronics: 30-day return window, original packaging required.
- Clothing: 60-day return window, unworn condition.
- Books: Non-refundable once opened, exchange only within 14 days.
- All refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

3.3 Bulk Order Discounts
Orders exceeding the following thresholds qualify for automatic bulk pricing:
- $500 to $999: 5% automatic discount.
- $1,000 to $2,499: 10% automatic discount.
- $2,500 to $4,999: 15% automatic discount.
- $5,000 and above: 20% discount, negotiable with sales manager.


4. DEPARTMENT & EMPLOYEE STRUCTURE

8. COMPANY POLICIES
---------------------

8.1 Remote Work Policy
ACME Corp operates on a hybrid model. Employees are expected to be in-office a minimum of 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday). Fully remote work may be approved by department heads for up to 4 weeks per year.

8.2 Expense Reimbursement
- Meals and entertainment: Up to $75 per person per event, receipts required.
- Travel: Economy class for domestic, business class for flights over 6 hours.
- Home office equipment: Up to $500 one-time allowance for new hires.

8.3 Performance Reviews
Annual reviews take place in December. Mid-year check-ins occur in June. Employees rated "Exceeds Expectations" are eligible for a merit increase of 5-10%. Promotions are budgeted annually and finalized in January.

8.4 Training & Development
Each employee receives an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, conferences, and certifications. Engineering team members receive an additional $500 for technical certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure, etc.).


9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
--------------------------------

Q: Who are the top-performing employees?
A: Top performers are recognised quarterly. The Sales department typically contributes the most sales transactions. Employees who exceed their quota by 20% or more receive a performance bonus of 8% of their base salary.

Q: What is the best-selling product?
A: The Laptop Pro 15" is consistently the highest-revenue product. Wireless Headphones and 4K Monitor are the second and third highest-revenue items respectively.

Q: What was revenue in Q1 2023?
A: Q1 2023 (January through March 2023) total revenue was approximately $580,000, representing 20% of the annual total for that year.

Q: Which department has the highest average salary?
A: Engineering has the highest average salary at approximately $93,000 across all levels. Finance is second at approximately $82,000.

Q: What is the company's return policy for electronics?
A: Electronics can be returned within 30 days of purchase in original packaging for a full refund. After 30 days, only warranty service is available. Refunds are processed in 5-7 business days.

Q: Are there any active projects in the HR department?
A: Yes, Project Epsilon is an active HR initiative focused on revamping the employee onboarding portal. It has a budget of $60,000 and aims to reduce new-hire time-to-productivity from 3 weeks to 10 days.

Q: What is the company's biggest market?
A: New York is ACME Corp's largest single market by customer count (28 customers) and by average order value ($340). The combined New York and California markets account for approximately 25% of total revenue.
